Privacy Policy

TCG Companion · Last updated 4 October 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

Summary

TCG Companion has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal detail, and we do not operate servers that store information about you. The only data leaving your device goes to the third-party services listed below.

Information stored on your device

  • Favourites and preferences — the cards you mark as favourites and your app settings are stored only on your device.
  • Cached card data — recently viewed sets and cards are cached so the app still works when the network is slow or unavailable.
  • Saved wallpapers — images you choose to save are written to your own photo library.

Deleting the app removes this data, except for wallpapers you saved to your photo library.

Camera

The card scanner uses your camera to read the name printed on a card. Text recognition runs entirely on your device using Apple's and Google's on-device machine-learning frameworks. Camera images are not uploaded, stored or shared. The recognised card name is used to search the card database, nothing more.

Advertising

The app displays advertising through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device information, such as a device advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads.

On first launch the app asks, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether you allow tracking across apps and websites. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using that identifier. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Google's handling of this data is described in its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Card data sources

Card, set and price information is requested from third-party databases — Pokémon TCG API and TCGdex — and news headlines are fetched from public feeds of community websites. These requests contain no personal information, though, like any internet request, the receiving service can see your IP address.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Decline or revoke app tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Revoke camera or photo access in Settings → TCG Companion.
  • Delete the app to remove all data it stored on your device.
