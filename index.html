About this app

TCG Companion is an unofficial fan app for collectors of the Pokémon Trading Card Game. It lets you browse sets, look up cards and market prices, scan a physical card with your camera, save card art as wallpapers, take quizzes and read community news.

Common questions

A card shows no price. Prices come from third-party market data. Some cards, especially very new or very obscure ones, have no price recorded yet.

Card images are missing for a set. Newly released sets sometimes appear in the card database before their images are published. They usually fill in within a few days.

The scanner doesn't recognise my card. The scanner reads the card name as text. Good lighting, a flat card and a steady hand help. Heavily worn or non-English cards may not be recognised.

Nothing loads. The app needs an internet connection. If one data source is down the app switches to a backup source automatically, which can take a few seconds.

I can't save a wallpaper. Saving needs permission to add photos. You can grant it in Settings → TCG Companion → Photos.
